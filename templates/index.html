How to Use Our System

Our Risk Management System is easy to use. Here you will find guidance for every step of the process to help you manage risks effectively.

Asset Inventory
Start by creating a list of all your critical assets. Include software, hardware, data, and people in your inventory.
Risk Assessment
Identify potential risks for each asset. Evaluate their probability and impact levels accurately.
Risk Analysis
Conduct detailed analysis of identified risks. Prioritize them and focus on the most critical ones.
Risk Response
Develop an action plan for each risk. Create mitigation strategies to reduce or eliminate risks.
User Management
Add users with different roles and responsibilities. Assign appropriate permissions for each user level.
Reporting
View detailed reports and dashboards. Track your progress and measure risk reduction efforts.
